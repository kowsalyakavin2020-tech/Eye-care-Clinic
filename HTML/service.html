Protocol

Five movements from first look to lifetime care.

Every discipline follows the same choreography. Hover a stage to open it; the sequence never skips a step.

Step 01

Intake & history

A structured conversation and a full medical, family and lifestyle history — the context every scan needs.

Step 02

Diagnose

Forty-one measurements across cornea, lens, retina and pressure, reviewed by two clinicians.

Step 03

Plan

A written pathway with options, timelines and costs — no procedure begins without your sign-off.

Step 04

Treat

Delivered by the same specialist who planned it, in a suite built for that single discipline.

Step 05

Protect

Scheduled reviews and lifetime imaging so we notice the smallest change early.

Milestones

Three decades, measured in clarity.

From one Rotterdam consulting room to a network of 38 clinics — every milestone began with a better way to see.

1994

The first consulting room

Founded in Rotterdam with a single slit lamp, one specialist and a stubborn belief that diagnosis should come before treatment.

  • Diagnostics
2003

Refractive programme opens

The first bladeless laser theatre joins the network, turning micron-level planning into everyday refractive surgery.
